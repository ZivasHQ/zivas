About Zivas

We believe education technology should make the work lighter.

Zivas is being built around a simple conviction: educators and institutions need dependable tools for the essential work of learning — not another layer of complexity.

Why we started

Education has enough complexity. The tools should not add to it.

Teaching involves far more than delivering a lesson. Educators organise courses, prepare materials, track attendance, collect assignments, review student work and communicate progress. Institutions coordinate the people and systems around that work.

Zivas exists to bring those essential workflows into one focused environment — making them easier to manage and easier to understand.

Our product principles

Focused by design. Useful in practice.

Clarity over clutter

Every workflow should be understandable without extensive training or unnecessary configuration.

People stay in control

Academic decisions belong to educators and institutions. Software should support their judgement.

Honest product claims

We distinguish available capabilities from work in progress and communicate limitations clearly.
