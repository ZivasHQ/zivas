About Zivas

Education needs clarity, not another layer of complexity.

We are building Zivas to help institutions and educators organise academic work and understand what is happening across the learning environment.

Why we started

Make academic activity visible, measurable and manageable.

Academic life is made up of connected responsibilities: organising courses, coordinating educators and learners, recording attendance, collecting student work, reviewing performance and following up when support is needed.

When these activities are scattered, it becomes harder for educators to stay on top of the work and for institutions to see the full picture. Zivas brings the essential operations into a focused workspace so people can act with better context.

Our mission is to make academic activity visible, measurable and manageable — giving educators and institutions the clarity to improve learning outcomes.

Our product principles

Focused by design. Accountable in practice.

01

Clarity over clutter

Build around real academic workflows instead of adding features for their own sake.

02

03
