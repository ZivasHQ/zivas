Privacy

Your information deserves careful handling.

This page is an overview, not a final legal privacy notice. The published policy must be reviewed and completed to reflect the actual data flows, processors, retention periods and applicable laws.

Information used to provide ZIVAS

Depending on how the service is used, ZIVAS may process account and contact details, institution and course information, attendance records, assignment submissions, grades, feedback and service communications.

Why information is processed

Information is used to provide and secure the learning service, support course and institutional workflows, communicate service updates and respond to support requests.

Access and sharing

Access to learner and academic records should be limited to authorised users and the institution or educator responsible for the relevant learning activity. ZIVAS should document its service providers and data-processing arrangements in the final policy.

Requests and questions

For privacy questions or requests, contact support.zivas@gmail.com. The final policy should specify the applicable rights, verification process and response timelines.

Before relying on this page, ZIVAS should obtain legal review and publish complete details about its data controller/processor roles, lawful bases, international transfers, retention, security practices and applicable data protection rights.
