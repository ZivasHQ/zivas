How ZIVAS works

A connected path through the everyday work of learning.

ZIVAS keeps the essential steps of teaching and learning connected, from organising a course to reviewing progress.

Book a demo ↗

Organise

Set up the learning workspace, courses, people and weekly activities. Institutional administrators coordinate the academic structure and access, while educators prepare the courses they are responsible for.

Engage

Share learning materials, communicate course updates, run classroom polls and record attendance. Students use their course workspace to find the activities and resources relevant to their learning.

Assess

Publish assignments, collect student submissions, review the work and release grades and feedback. Educators remain responsible for evaluating work and deciding when results are ready to publish.

Understand

Review course activity, attendance, submissions and published performance information. These records help educators and academic leaders identify where follow-up or learner support may be needed.

How the roles fit together

Institutional administrators oversee the wider academic environment and coordinate people, departments and courses. Educators manage the day-to-day teaching activities within their courses. Students access course resources, complete activities, submit assignments and follow feedback and released results.

The aim is not to add more steps to academic work. It is to keep the essential activity connected, so each role can focus on the information and tasks relevant to it.

Start with the workflows you need

Every institution has its own academic structure and routines. A guided ZIVAS walkthrough can help map those requirements to the platform's available workflows and clarify any capabilities that are still in development.
