From sign-up to graded work, without the setup project

Zivas is designed so a lecturer can go from nothing to a running course in one sitting.

  1. Create your account

    Choose lecturer, then whether you teach independently or through an institution. Registration takes four short steps.

  2. Build your course

    Add a course and its weeks. Each week gets its own assignments, resources and quizzes.

  3. Invite students

    Students join your class from their own portal.

  4. Teach and collect

    Take attendance with a QR code, run a live quiz and publish assignments.

  5. Grade and review

    Release scores, watch class trends and export results to Excel.
