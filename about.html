Why we built Zivas

Learning platforms kept getting bigger while teachers’ days kept getting longer. Zivas starts from the opposite end: automate the repetitive classroom exercises and keep everything else out of the way.

We are not trying to be another bloated LMS. We are building the weekly toolkit a lecturer actually reaches for: coursework, attendance, quizzes, progress and clear communication with students.

How we work

Teacher time comes first

If a feature does not save a lecturer time, it does not ship.

Plain over clever

Clear screens and simple setup beat configuration options.

Honest about the roadmap

Live means live. Anything else is labelled coming soon.
