A focused academic management platform

Connect the people, courses and activity behind academic progress.

Academic management involves more than keeping a list of students. Institutions need a clear view of the departments and courses they run, the educators and learners connected to them, and the activity taking place throughout each term. ZIVAS brings these core records and workflows into one institutional workspace.

Administrators can organise departments, manage lecturer and student accounts, associate courses with the right academic structure and review institution-wide indicators. Educators work within their courses to share resources, manage assignments, record attendance and review student work. Students use their course spaces to access learning materials, submit assignments and follow released grades and feedback.

This is a focused approach to academic operations. It is designed to support the work around teaching and learning without turning the platform into an unnecessarily complicated collection of unrelated modules.
