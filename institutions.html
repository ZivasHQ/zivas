Add lecturers and students, organise departments and courses, and manage your plan. Everyone you add gets the same lightweight tools.

Students and lecturers

Create accounts inside your school’s workspace with a temporary password, then edit or remove people as they join and leave.

Departments

Set up departments with a name, code and status, then keep courses and people organised under them.

Courses

Create a course with its title, code, lecturer, department, start date and length in weeks, and switch it on when it is ready.

School settings

Keep your school’s name, contact email, address, phone, website, academic year and timezone up to date.

Plan and payments

See your current plan and subscription status, renew online and review every payment you have made.

Lecturers do not pay individually

Lecturers tied to your school are covered by your school’s plan.

Departments, ready in minutes

Add a department once and use it everywhere: when you create students, assign lecturers and set up courses.

Courses with the right lecturer

Pick the lecturer and department, set the start date and number of weeks, and the course appears in that lecturer’s dashboard.

One place for your school profile

Your workspace, current plan and subscription status sit alongside your school details, so nothing is scattered.

Plans for schools

Schools choose Standard or Enterprise from the dashboard and pay online in naira.

Standard is ₦20,000 per month. Enterprise is ₦240,000 per year and includes AI grading when it launches. The Zivas team approves Enterprise before you can pay for it.
