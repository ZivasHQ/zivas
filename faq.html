Is Zivas a traditional LMS?

Zivas is positioned as a focused learning operations platform, bringing together essential teaching, assessment and academic coordination workflows without unnecessary complexity.

Who is Zivas for?

Independent tutors, lecturers, students and institutional administrators.

Does Zivas support institutions?

Yes. Institutional workspaces support administration of departments, courses, lecturers and students, alongside academic performance views.

Can students submit assignments?

Assignment submission is part of the course workflow. Supported file types and preview behaviour depend on the current production configuration.

Is AI grading generally available?

AI-assisted grading should not be treated as generally available until it is enabled, validated and confirmed in the production application.

Are international payments available?

The current documented payment integration is Paystack. Contact Zivas about international billing requirements; do not assume local-currency checkout is available.

How do I join the beta?

Use the early access request page to tell us your role, organisation and what you would like to test.

How can an institution arrange a demo?

Contact the team through the early access page to discuss a guided walkthrough or pilot.
