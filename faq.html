Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about ZIVAS.

Evaluating ZIVAS for your institution? Arrange a guided walkthrough with our team.

What is ZIVAS?

ZIVAS is a focused academic operations platform that helps educators and institutions coordinate courses, academic activity and performance visibility.

Who is it designed for?

Schools, colleges and universities are the primary audience. Independent educators and students also have role-based workspaces.

Is ZIVAS a traditional all-in-one LMS?

No. ZIVAS focuses on the operational work around teaching, assessment, attendance and academic oversight rather than accumulating every possible education feature.

Does ZIVAS include live classes?

No. Live video classes are intentionally outside the current product scope.

Can institutions manage departments and courses?

Yes. Institutional administrators can manage departments, courses, lecturer accounts, student accounts and school settings.

Does ZIVAS support attendance?

Yes. Current workflows include QR and OTP student check-in, attendance records and lecturer attendance monitoring.

Is AI grading available?

Not currently in production. Manual grading, feedback and controlled grade publication are available; AI-assisted grading remains disabled while it is prepared.

Does ZIVAS work offline?

Uploaded resources can be saved to a device for offline access. YouTube and Google Drive links still require internet, and broader offline functionality continues to evolve.

How can my institution see ZIVAS?

Request a guided demo. We will follow up to understand your institution and arrange a walkthrough.

Where can I ask about plans and deployment?

Contact the team directly. Public pricing is not displayed on this website; institutional requirements can be discussed with ZIVAS.
