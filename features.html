Everything a lecturer needs, nothing they do not

Zivas covers the weekly rhythm of teaching. Every feature below is live today unless it says coming soon.

Book a demo

For lecturers

Week-by-week courses

Every course is laid out by week. Switch weeks and see that week’s assignments, resources and activity.

Assignments

Publish coursework for a given week, collect submissions, flag late work and grade with a score out of the total.

Resources

Keep learning materials with the week they belong to.

Quizzes and polls

Create quizzes and polls, attach them to a week and take them live in class.

QR attendance

Start a live session and show a QR code. Students scan to check in; a fallback code is always available.

Student insight

Open any student to see average score, submissions, late work, attendance and on-time streak.

Excel export

Download course scores as an Excel file in one click.

Discussion

A discussion space for each course so questions stay with the class.

Notifications

Grade changes and class updates are delivered to students automatically.

Know every student at a glance

Scores, submissions, attendance and streaks sit on one screen, so you can spot who needs help before the exam.

For students

My courses

Join a class and see all your courses with the current week.

Weekly view

See assignments week by week and submit work in the same place.

Progress and targets

Set a target grade and see how far you are from it as scores are published.

Performance charts

Weekly averages, on-time streak and attendance rate, with a clear change from last week.

Notifications

Every published or updated grade arrives as a notification you can review later.

For school administrators

Accounts for everyone

Create student and lecturer accounts in your school’s workspace and manage them later.

Departments and courses

Organise departments, then create courses and assign them to lecturers.

School settings

Maintain your school profile, academic year and timezone.

Plan and payments

Renew your plan online and review your payment history.
