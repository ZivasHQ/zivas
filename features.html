Platform capabilities

The essential workflows of academic operations, connected.

Explore the capabilities currently represented in the production application. Availability can depend on role, institution configuration and service readiness.

Available now

Current platform capabilities

These capabilities have corresponding production application pages and/or backend workflows.

Institution management

Institution profile and settings, departments, course administration, lecturer and student accounts, and role-based access.

Course workspaces

Course organisation, weekly learning activities, resources, course enrolment and learning progress.

Assignments and submissions

Create and publish assignments, set deadlines, collect student work and manage submissions. File preview depends on file type and configured storage.

Manual grading and feedback

Review submissions, score work, provide feedback and control when grades are published.

Automated quizzes

Create timed course quizzes with multiple-choice questions, multiple options, attempt limits and lecturer-controlled publication.

Classroom polls

Run live course polls and review student responses during teaching activity.

Student attendance

Open attendance sessions with QR check-in and a code-based fallback; review recorded attendance.

Lecturer attendance

Record and monitor lecturer class attendance, including attendance status and related course activity.

Timetables

Upload and review timetable files, publish class schedules and provide weekly timetable views for relevant roles.

Course discussions

Use course discussion threads and replies, with accepted answers and activity notifications.

PDF learning annotations

Annotate course PDF resources with private, page-specific notes.

Progress targets

Students can set course progress targets and follow their released progress summaries.

Anonymous course feedback

Students can submit course feedback anonymously. Aggregate reporting is withheld until at least five responses are received.

Performance intelligence

Review attendance, submission completion, on-time submissions, published grade averages and course-level performance detail.

Notifications and reports

In-app and email notifications, weekly course reports and Excel assessment score exports for academic follow-up.

In development

What is being built next

These are not advertised as generally available production capabilities.

Not currently available

AI-assisted grading

The production AI grading endpoints are disabled while the grading engine is prepared. Manual grading and grade publication remain available.

Future consideration

Offline and low-data access

Uploaded file resources can now be saved to a device for offline access. Broader offline functionality, including a more complete offline course experience, remains in development.

A deliberate product boundary

Focused on academic operations, not feature accumulation.

ZIVAS is not being positioned as a live-class or video-conferencing platform. The focus is on organising academic activity and making it easier to understand.
