For students

Know what is next. Keep track of your progress.

ZIVAS gives learners a clear place to follow their courses, access learning activities, submit work and review academic progress.

Your learning activity in one place.

  • Access course materials and weekly activities
  • See assignments and submission status
  • Submit coursework and review published grades
  • Follow attendance and progress summaries
  • Receive relevant course notifications

Make progress visible.

Progress views help students understand submitted work, performance trends and attendance records.

When an educator releases a grade or feedback, students can return to the relevant course activity to review the outcome and understand what has been assessed.

Know what needs your attention

Use the course workspace to check learning materials, weekly activities and assignment instructions. Submission status helps you keep track of work that has been sent to your educator and work that still needs attention.

Review feedback and released results

After an educator reviews your work and publishes the result, you can follow the grade and feedback made available for that activity. Attendance and progress views provide additional context about your participation across the course.

One place for your course activity

ZIVAS is designed to keep the everyday parts of learning connected: finding resources, understanding tasks, submitting work and following progress. Your institution or educator determines the courses and activities available to your account.
